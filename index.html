Hovel Ideas

Novel ideas with a soul.

Hovel Ideas is a creative workshop, idea-building company, and community for people trying to turn rough sparks into real things: books, tools, inventions, safety concepts, and practical projects that help somebody.

Email Tom See the work The community

Good ideas should not die because people do not have the money, polish, connections, or perfect vocabulary to explain them yet.

This is where rough ideas get a workbench.

What we’re building

Some are technology tools. Some are safety concepts. Some are books. Some are inventions. The common thread is simple: build something that helps.

01

SafeSchool

A student support and school safety concept built around quiet communication, compassion, accountability, and help before the crisis gets louder.

02

Hovel Editor

A structured editorial tool for serious writing, manuscripts, reports, essays, and professional documents. Honest feedback without sanding the soul out of the work.

03

The Ideanator

A guided idea-development tool for the messy beginning: business ideas, product concepts, pitches, grants, speeches, essays, and raw thoughts that need shape.

04

WILL’d

A legacy and memory-centered concept built around preserving meaning, intent, and human connection beyond ordinary paperwork.

05

Memory Garden Lamp

A remembrance concept built around grief, light, ritual, and presence. A gentle physical answer to memory, loss, and love that stays.

06

Halo Wrist Lamp

A hands-free lighting concept for workers, makers, mechanics, and problem-solvers who need the light to follow the work.

07

Books by Tom Mitchell

Novels, essays, and stories published through Tom Mitchell and Hovel Ideas: grief, faith, family, absurdity, tenderness, and the strange holy mess of being human.

08

Founder tools

Practical idea-shaping systems built for people who have more vision than vocabulary and more grit than polish.

09

Community

A place for like-minded people carrying napkin sketches, half-built dreams, grief-made visions, and practical fixes for problems the world keeps ignoring.

Build something that helps.
Build something with a soul.

Not slick for the sake of slick. Not technology for the sake of technology. Not hustle culture with a better font.

The Hovel

Hovel Ideas is a gathering place for writers, teachers, parents, founders, builders, inventors, students, oddballs, and anyone carrying an idea that might help somebody.

It is where lived experience, stubborn hope, practical imagination, and useful technology sit at the same crooked table.
